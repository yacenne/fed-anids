# Academic References for Fed-ANIDS

This document provides authentic, peer-reviewed academic citations for the **Fed-ANIDS** project, establishing foundational credibility across top IEEE and Elsevier publications.

## 🎯 Slide 8 Replacement: Key Academic & Dataset References

Use these exact citations for **Slide 8** of your B.Tech Project presentation and project report:

1. **T. D. Nguyen, S. Marchal, M. Miettinen, H. Fereidooni, N. Asokan, and A.-R. Sadeghi**, *"DÏoT: A Federated Self-learning Anomaly Detection System for IoT,"* in **Proceedings of the 2019 IEEE 39th International Conference on Distributed Computing Systems (ICDCS)**, pp. 756–767, 2019. DOI: [10.1109/ICDCS.2019.00080](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICDCS.2019.00080).
   > *Role:* The seminal IEEE paper pioneering decentralized federated self-learning anomaly detection with autoencoders.

2. **V. Mothukuri, P. Khare, R. M. Parizi, S. Pouriyeh, A. Dehghantanha, and G. Srivastava**, *"Federated-learning-based anomaly detection for IoT security attacks,"* **IEEE Internet of Things Journal**, vol. 9, no. 4, pp. 2545–2554, 2022. DOI: [10.1109/JIOT.2021.3077803](https://doi.org/10.1109/JIOT.2021.3077803).
   > *Role:* Establishes federated proactive anomaly detection architectures for distributed edge networks in a flagship IEEE journal.

3. **B. McMahan, E. Moore, D. Ramage, S. Hampson, and B. A. y Arcas**, *"Communication-efficient learning of deep networks from decentralized data,"* in **Proceedings of the 20th International Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Statistics (AISTATS)**, PMLR, vol. 54, pp. 1273–1282, 2017.
   > *Role:* The foundational paper introducing Federated Averaging (FedAvg).

4. **T. Li, A. K. Sahu, M. Zaheer, M. Sanjabi, A. Talwalkar, and V. Smith**, *"Federated optimization in heterogeneous networks,"* in **Proceedings of Machine Learning and Systems (MLSys)**, vol. 2, pp. 429–450, 2020.
   > *Role:* Proposes FedProx, introducing the proximal regularizer term $\frac{\mu}{2}\|w - w_t\|^2$ to handle statistical and system heterogeneity.

5. **I. Sharafaldin, A. Habibi Lashkari, and A. A. Ghorbani**, *"Toward generating a new intrusion detection dataset and intrusion traffic characterization,"* in **Proceedings of the 4th International Conference on Information Systems Security and Privacy (ICISSP)**, pp. 108–116, 2018.
   > *Role:* The official benchmark paper introducing the CIC-IDS2017 dataset.

6. **W. Wang, M. Zhu, X. Zeng, X. Ye, and Y. Sheng**, *"Malware traffic classification using convolutional neural network for representation learning,"* in **2017 International Conference on Information Networking (ICOIN)**, pp. 712–717, IEEE, 2017.
   > *Role:* Introduces benchmark traffic classification representation methodologies.

## 📚 Supporting References on Data Quality & Anomaly Detection

7. **G. Engelen, V. Rimmer, and W. Joosen**, *"Troubleshooting an intrusion detection dataset: the CICIDS2017 case study,"* in **2021 IEEE Security and Privacy Workshops (SPW)**, pp. 7–12, IEEE, 2021.
   > *Role:* Identifies flow construction anomalies in CICFlowMeter and provides clean flow extraction tools.

8. **L. Liu, G. Engelen, T. Lynar, D. Essam, and W. Joosen**, *"Error prevalence in NIDS datasets: A case study on CIC-ids-2017 and CSE-cic-IDS-2018,"* in **2022 IEEE Conference on Communications and Network Security (CNS)**, pp. 254–262, IEEE, 2022.
   > *Role:* Analyzes feature errors and guides proper label assignment for CIC-IDS2017/2018 flows.

9. **Y. Meidan, M. Bohadana, Y. Mathov, Y. Mirsky, A. Shabtai, D. Breitenbacher, and Y. Elovici**, *"N-BaIoT—Network-based detection of IoT botnet attacks using deep autoencoders,"* **IEEE Pervasive Computing**, vol. 17, no. 3, pp. 12–22, 2018.
   > *Role:* Provides the statistical thresholding formulation $\text{thr} = \text{mean}(MSE) + \text{std}(MSE)$ used in network anomaly detection.

10. **A. Tabassum, A. Erbad, W. Lebda, A. Mohamed, and M. Guizani**, *"FEDGAN-IDS: Privacy-preserving IDS using GAN and federated learning,"* **Computer Communications**, vol. 192, pp. 299–310, Elsevier, 2022.
    > *Role:* The primary state-of-the-art GAN baseline compared against Fed-ANIDS.
